<h2 id="what">What moderators are</h2>
<p>Splitstone's <a href="https://discord.gg/wxDcZ8NGWt">Discord</a> has volunteer community moderators. They are players who help keep the server in order. They are not part of the Splitstone team.</p>
<h2 id="can">What a moderator can and can't do</h2>
<p>Moderators have three steps. Each step can do everything the one before it can.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Trial Moderator</b> (the first 2 to 4 weeks): deletes messages that break the <a href="../rules.html">rules</a>, and times a member out for up to 24 hours.</li>
<li><b>Moderator:</b> can also suspend a member, with a 1-week timeout, until the Splitstone team reviews it.</li>
<li><b>Senior Moderator:</b> can also kick, and lift other moderators' timeouts and suspensions.</li>
<li><b>Nobody but the team bans.</b> The team also reviews every suspension and decides who moves up a step.</li>
<li><b>Doesn't see:</b> reports, appeals or support requests. Those stay with the team.</li>
<li><b>In the game:</b> a moderator has no powers at all. Bans, cheating and match results are the team's.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="who">Who can apply</h2>
<ul>
<li>You are 16 or older.</li>
<li>You have had no Splitstone sanction in the last 6 months.</li>
<li>You are active in the Discord.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="apply">How to apply</h2>
<p>Applications are open. The team may close them for a while when it has more applications than it can answer; the <b>#mod-applications</b> channel in the Discord says whether they are open.</p>
<ol class="steps">
<li><span><b>Start a private thread in #mod-applications.</b>Only you and the Splitstone team can see it.</span></li>
<li><span><b>Tell us about you.</b>Your age, your Minecraft name, your time zone, how many hours a week you can be around, and any moderating you've done before.</span></li>
<li><span><b>Talk with the team.</b>If we'd like to go further, one of us talks with you, by voice or text.</span></li>
<li><span><b>Start your trial.</b>New moderators start as Trial Moderators for 2 to 4 weeks.</span></li>
</ol>
<h2 id="after">While you moderate</h2>
<ul>
<li>Every moderator gets free Supporter while they volunteer (<a href="supporting.html">Supporting Splitstone</a>). Moderators and Senior Moderators also get one free item of their choice from each item shop batch and a moderators-only cosmetic that can't be bought.</li>
<li>Senior Moderators also talk with the team each month about the rules and the community, and suggest Trial Moderators to move up.</li>
<li>After a year, or when you step down in good standing, you keep a veteran cosmetic for good.</li>
<li>A moderator who isn't active for 21 days loses the role, and can apply again.</li>
</ul>
