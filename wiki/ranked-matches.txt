<h2 id="modes">Modes</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Ranked 1v1:</b> you against one opponent, each in your own copy of the same seed.</li>
<li><b>Ranked 2v2:</b> two teams of two, each team sharing one world.</li>
<li><b>Co-op:</b> two players share a world. Co-op is unrated: no Elo, rank or leaderboard.</li>
<li><b>2&times; speed:</b> the one game mode at launch. Game modes change how the game runs, not the goal of the run.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="start">Before the start</h2>
<p>Once a match is found it can't be cancelled or declined, so nobody can dodge. Two 10-second countdowns follow: one when the match is found and one on the loading screen. Nobody can see the seed until all 20 seconds have passed.</p>
<p>If your game crashes or fails to load before the start, you reconnect automatically and the match goes on.</p>
<h2 id="during">During the run</h2>
<ul>
<li>Matches start on Easy, and you can switch to Hard. Choosing Normal or Peaceful gives Hard, and once on Hard you stay on Hard. If you die, you respawn and keep playing. Hardcore is allowed only in game modes and single-player worlds.</li>
<li>You can reset your world at any time, with no cooldown.</li>
<li>You see your opponent's advancements live, which also helps players spot cheating.</li>
<li>The seed stays secret: <b>/seed</b> is off, the seed is stored encrypted, and opening to LAN is blocked in 1v1. There is no copy-seed button: after the match, your saved world holds the seed, so you can explore it and read the seed there.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="votes">Votes and forfeits</h2>
<p>An on-screen countdown shows when each of these unlocks.</p>
<div class="tw"><table class="wt"><thead><tr><th>Option</th><th>When</th><th>How it works</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>Reroll</td><td>Until 3:00 in the top two ranks, until 4:30 below</td><td>A new seed. Press Reroll in the match menu, or hold <b>F7</b>, to turn on your Reroll light; the match restarts on a new seed as soon as both lights are on. Up to <b>3</b> per player.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Draw vote</td><td>From 4:30</td><td>Both players have 30 seconds to agree.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Forfeit</td><td>From 4:30, or 3:30 from Obsidian up, each player by their own rank</td><td>Ends the match as a loss for you.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p>Pressing a vote's button starts the vote or agrees to your opponent's, and a vote nobody agrees to runs out. You can turn your Reroll light off again.</p>
<h2 id="leaving">Leaving and disconnects</h2>
<ul>
<li>If you disconnect or crash mid-match, you reconnect automatically with your progress kept, but you forfeit if you're not back within <b>4</b> minutes.</li>
<li>If you are away from the keyboard for <b>4</b> minutes, you forfeit automatically.</li>
<li>Leaving a match that then ends in a forfeit counts the same however it happened, because Splitstone can't tell a crash from a closed game. Penalties grow if it keeps happening.</li>
<li>Three forfeits in a row give a 3-minute queue ban, and the ban grows with each further forfeit in a row.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="winning">Winning and timing</h2>
<p>The first player to enter the exit portal after the dragon dies wins. There is no time limit.</p>
<p>Every match is timed in real time (RTA) only. Resetting your world doesn't restart the clock: your time counts from the match start.</p>
<p>If your opponent forfeits, you can keep playing; a small notice briefly offers to leave. A run you finish counts exactly as if they hadn't forfeited.</p>
<h2 id="teams">2v2 and co-op</h2>
<ul>
<li>A team wins as soon as its first player finishes.</li>
<li>A teammate who dies respawns, and one who disconnects rejoins, as in any shared Minecraft world.</li>
<li>If the host's game drops, the host has 3 minutes to reopen the world for their teammate. The clock keeps running.</li>
<li>A team forfeits only together: one teammate starts the forfeit vote and the other confirms within 30 seconds. No answer counts as no.</li>
<li>In 2v2, Reroll and draw votes pass with <b>3</b> of the <b>4</b> players.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="after">After the match</h2>
<ul>
<li>The results screen offers a rematch, finding another match, or the main menu. A rematch is allowed once, both players must accept, and in ranked it counts for Elo.</li>
<li>You can like or dislike the seed. A seed both players dislike leaves the pool.</li>
<li>In 2v2 and co-op you can endorse a teammate as a good teammate, a good sport or helpful.</li>
<li>The match stays in your public match history for good: result, times, opponents, seed and bastion type, Elo change, how it ended, deaths and resets, and the replay while it is kept (<a href="replays-and-spectating.html">Replays</a>).</li>
</ul>
