<h2 id="friends">Friends</h2>
<ul>
<li>Add friends by searching their username, from their profile, or from the screen after a match.</li>
<li>When you first launch Splitstone, you choose who can send you friend requests: everyone, friends of friends and people you've played with, or nobody. You can change it later.</li>
<li>A friends list holds up to 999 friends.</li>
<li>Friends see whether you're online and what you're doing, such as playing ranked or practising.</li>
<li>You can invite a friend to your party, a private room, your 2v2 or co-op world, or to watch or coach your match.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="parties">Parties</h2>
<ul>
<li>A party holds up to 64 players. More than two play together in <a href="private-rooms.html">private rooms</a>, as a free-for-all or in teams of two.</li>
<li>One leader queues for the party, can promote and kick, and runs a ready check before queueing.</li>
<li>You can party with people who aren't your friends. After a match with random teammates, "stay together" keeps you in a party.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="blocking">Blocking</h2>
<ul>
<li>Blocking hides that player's messages, friend requests, invites and voice, and they're never your random 2v2 or co-op teammate.</li>
<li>You can still meet them as an opponent. In that match they can't chat to you, and names stay hidden until it ends.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="chat">Chat</h2>
<ul>
<li>There are direct messages, party chat, team chat in 2v2 and co-op, chat between opponents in a match, and chat in public rooms. Spectators can't message the player they watch.</li>
<li>A filter for swearing and slurs is on by default. Players 18 or older can turn it off; players who said they are 13&ndash;17 can&rsquo;t.</li>
<li>Chat is kept for 30 days, for moderation only.</li>
<li>You can mute a player, or everyone for one match.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="voice">Voice</h2>
<p>Four voice channels at launch:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Party voice.</b></li>
<li><b>Team voice</b> in 2v2 and co-op.</li>
<li><b>Coach voice</b> between a coach and their player.</li>
<li><b>Room voice</b> for everyone in a private room, if the host turns it on.</li>
</ul>
<p>Opponents can only talk to each other in private rooms. In ranked they have text chat only. Voice is never recorded. Push-to-talk is the default.</p>
<p>If your Microsoft account settings turn off chat or multiplayer, Splitstone turns off voice for you and tells you to change the setting with Microsoft.</p>
<h2 id="hub">Is there a hub or SMP?</h2>
<p>No. There is no hub world at launch, no plan for one yet, and no SMP world.</p>
