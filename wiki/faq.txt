<h2 id="basics">The basics</h2>
<div class="qa">
<details id="about"><summary>What is Splitstone?</summary><div><p>An independent Minecraft speedrunning platform: ranked 1v1 and 2v2 races, co-op, private rooms, practice tools, replays, seasons and events. It runs its own matchmaking, ratings and service. It is not an official Minecraft product and is not run by MCSR Ranked.</p></div></details>
<details id="free"><summary>Is it free?</summary><div><p>Yes. Playing ranked, the practice tools and replay analysis are free, and so is the whole battle pass at launch. Optional monthly tiers support the project (<a href="supporting.html">Supporting Splitstone</a>).</p></div></details>
<details id="paying"><summary>Does paying help me in ranked?</summary><div><p>No. Paid tiers add profile, private-room and social extras. Rank marks such as gold, the crown, medals and the lava glow can only be earned, and bought cosmetics never appear on leaderboard rows.</p></div></details>
<details id="release"><summary>When can I play?</summary><div><p>Downloads aren't released yet, and no release date has been announced.</p></div></details>
</div>
<h2 id="setup">Install and setup</h2>
<div class="qa">
<details id="launchers"><summary>Which launchers work?</summary><div><p>Prism, MultiMC, the Modrinth App and the official Minecraft Launcher. Prism, MultiMC and the Modrinth App all use the same Modrinth pack (<a href="install.html#launchers">Install</a>).</p></div></details>
<details id="curseforge"><summary>Why not CurseForge?</summary><div><p>CurseForge packs may only include mods hosted on CurseForge or on its approved list, and most speedrun-legal builds are neither.</p></div></details>
<details id="mac-linux"><summary>Does it work on Mac and Linux?</summary><div><p>Yes. The mod and both packs are made for Windows, macOS and Linux. On macOS the pack uses Sodium's Mac build.</p></div></details>
<details id="mcsr-ranked"><summary>Can I keep MCSR Ranked installed?</summary><div><p>Yes, in a separate instance. Splitstone needs its own instance because Ranked won't start next to mods that aren't on the speedrun.com legal list (<a href="install.html#own-instance">why</a>).</p></div></details>
<details id="own-mods"><summary>Can I use my own mods and tools?</summary><div><p>You can use exactly the mods on the speedrun.com legal list, and Splitstone checks each file against it. Fabric API can't be installed on its own; Splitstone's mod brings the parts it needs. External tools follow speedrun.com's list, such as its approved calculators like Ninjabrain Bot.</p><p>During a match, reset mods (Atum, SeedQueue, WorldPreview) can't reset or create worlds, and SeedQueue pauses background generation. StandardSettings still applies your settings. Outside matches they all work normally.</p></div></details>
<details id="old-version"><summary>I'm on an old version and can't connect.</summary><div><p>Only the latest version can connect. Let the in-game updater install the new version.</p></div></details>
</div>
<h2 id="account">Your account</h2>
<div class="qa">
<details id="no-password"><summary>Do I need a Splitstone account or password?</summary><div><p>No. Your Minecraft account is your Splitstone account, one per Minecraft account. There is no separate sign-up (<a href="install.html#sign-in">how sign-in works</a>).</p></div></details>
<details id="website-sign-in"><summary>Can I sign in on the website?</summary><div><p>No. The website has no sign-in. It shows public profiles, and the shop asks for the Minecraft username of whoever the purchase is for.</p></div></details>
<details id="alts"><summary>Can I use alt accounts?</summary><div><p>Yes, there is no limit. Using an alt to dodge a ban or to boost someone's Elo is against the rules and is punished. A ban covers one Minecraft account.</p></div></details>
<details id="name-change"><summary>How do I change my name?</summary><div><p>Change your Minecraft username. Your Splitstone name always follows it, and your history, Elo and items stay with your account. Past names aren't shown on profiles.</p></div></details>
<details id="twitch-youtube"><summary>How do I link Twitch or YouTube?</summary><div><p>From inside the game. Linked accounts show on your profile, and you can hide them.</p></div></details>
<details id="age"><summary>How old do I have to be?</summary><div><p>13 or older. To buy, you must be 16 or older, or a parent or guardian buying for the player.</p></div></details>
<details id="delete-account"><summary>What happens if I delete my account?</summary><div><p>Your past matches stay, so opponents' histories and leaderboards stay correct, and your name on them is shown faded. You can ask for your name to be removed from past matches. Deleting your account also deletes its items, coins and tier, and cancels its subscription.</p></div></details>
</div>
<h2 id="buying">Buying</h2>
<div class="qa">
<details id="cancel"><summary>How do I cancel a tier?</summary><div><p>Online, any time. You keep the tier until the end of the month you paid for, and you get an email before each renewal (<a href="supporting.html">Supporting Splitstone</a>).</p></div></details>
<details id="refunds"><summary>Can I get a refund?</summary><div><p>Purchases are final, except where the law gives a refund. EU and UK buyers can return a coin pack within 14 days of buying it, while none of it is spent, and get a full refund for a tier cancelled within 14 days of subscribing. An item bought with coins can't be returned once you've confirmed the purchase; you get an email confirming it.</p></div></details>
<details id="banned-purchases"><summary>What happens to my purchases if I'm banned?</summary><div><p>A temporary ban pauses your tier's payments until it ends. A permanent ban cancels the tier and removes the account's items and coins.</p></div></details>
</div>
<h2 id="problems">Problems and help</h2>
<div class="qa">
<details id="report-bug"><summary>How do I report a bug?</summary><div><p>Use the "Report a bug" button in the game.</p></div></details>
<details id="crash-report"><summary>My game crashed. What happens to the crash report?</summary><div><p>The next time you start Splitstone, it asks whether to send the crash report. Nothing is sent unless you say yes. Logs go to <a href="https://mclo.gs" rel="noopener">mclo.gs</a>, which removes file paths, usernames, IP addresses and tokens, and Splitstone keeps only the link. Anyone with the link can read the log, and it expires 90 days after it was last viewed.</p></div></details>
<details id="crash-in-match"><summary>I crashed during a match. Did I lose?</summary><div><p>No. You reconnect automatically and keep your progress, even before the match has started. A player who is away from the keyboard for 5 minutes forfeits (<a href="ranked-matches.html#leaving">leaving and disconnects</a>).</p></div></details>
<details id="sign-in"><summary>Why can't I sign in?</summary><div><p>Check that you're on the latest version. Sign-in also needs Microsoft's service, so it fails while Microsoft is down.</p></div></details>
<details id="in-line"><summary>Why am I seeing "you're in line"?</summary><div><p>Players are arriving faster than the service can take them, and you'll get in as soon as there's room.</p></div></details>
<details id="report-player"><summary>How do I report a player?</summary><div><p>In the game, from their profile, a chat message or the match. The founders review reports with help from automated tools.</p></div></details>
<details id="appeal"><summary>How do I appeal a ban or mute?</summary><div><p>In the game or through a form on the website. The founders review every appeal themselves.</p></div></details>
<details id="help"><summary>Where do I get help?</summary><div><p>By email to <a href="mailto:contact@splitstonemc.com">contact@splitstonemc.com</a>, or in the game.</p></div></details>
</div>
