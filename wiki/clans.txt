<h2 id="basics">The basics</h2>
<ul>
<li>A player can be in one clan at a time, and a clan holds up to 30 members.</li>
<li>Your clan sits in the dashboard's side panel, under your party. It opens the clan's screen: Overview, Chat, Requests (for Elders and above) and Settings. Outside a clan, the same place lets you make, find and join one.</li>
<li>Clans are free. There is no clan leaderboard or clan score yet.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="roles">Roles</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Member:</b> posts in the chat when allowed, and can leave.</li>
<li><b>Elder:</b> also invites players, answers requests to join, and can remove a Member at most once every 20 minutes.</li>
<li><b>Co-leader:</b> also removes Members and Elders without a wait, gives roles up to Co-leader, changes the settings and moderates the chat.</li>
<li><b>Leader:</b> does everything, and can hand the clan over.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="joining">Making and joining</h2>
<ul>
<li>Making a clan is one form: the name (4 to 24 letters or digits) and the tag (2 to 5 capital letters or digits), who gets in, and the shield. Names and tags are unique, and offensive ones are refused.</li>
<li>A clan's joining setting is Ask to join (the default), Open, or Invite only. It can also set a minimum Ranked 1v1 Elo and a region: North America, Europe, Asia, Oceania, the Middle East, or any region.</li>
<li>An invite lets a player in whatever the joining setting and the minimum Elo. Nobody can invite a player they blocked or who blocked them.</li>
<li>The shield is one of 8 emblems on one of 8 colours, all free, picked by the Leader or a Co-leader.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="chat">Clan chat</h2>
<ul>
<li>The chat is off when a clan is made. The Leader or a Co-leader turns it on and chooses who may post: everyone, Elders and above, or Leaders and Co-leaders only.</li>
<li>They can delete messages and mute anyone below them. Clan chat is kept for 30 days for moderation, like party chat.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="leader">When the Leader is away</h2>
<ul>
<li>A Leader away 21 and 32 days gets a warning when they sign in, and every member's clan card shows how long the Leader has been away.</li>
<li>At 35 days the clan passes to the longest-serving Co-leader. With no Co-leader, nothing changes.</li>
<li>The Leader hands the clan over before leaving. The last member to leave closes it, and the Leader can also disband it; its name and tag are free again at once.</li>
</ul>
