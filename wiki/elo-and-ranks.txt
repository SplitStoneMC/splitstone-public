<h2 id="elo">Your Elo</h2>
<p>Elo is a number that rises when you win and falls when you lose. Beating a higher-rated player moves it more than beating a lower-rated one.</p>
<ul>
<li>You start at <b>1000</b> until your placement matches set your rating.</li>
<li>The <b>K-factor</b> is the most one match can move you. At launch it is <b>80</b> for your first 10 matches after placement, counted again each season, so new ratings settle quickly, then <b>40</b>, and <b>30</b> from your 100th match of the season, so a settled rating doesn't swing as much on one good or bad week.</li>
<li>Once enough people play, Splitstone drops the faster start: K is 40 for everyone, and still 30 from your 100th match of the season.</li>
<li>A draw counts as half a win: equal players don't move, and the lower-rated player gains a little.</li>
<li>A gap of more than 400 Elo counts as 400.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="2v2">2v2</h2>
<p>2v2 gives every player a separate 2v2 Elo. After each match it moves by your team's average Elo, the same average that matchmaking uses. It works exactly as 1v1 does: the same starting Elo, K, placement, ranks and decay.</p>
<h2 id="placement">Placement matches</h2>
<p>New players, and every player after a season reset, play <b>5</b> placement matches. Each one scores how far you got, not how fast, and your starting Elo is the average of the five:</p>

<h2 id="inactive">Taking a break</h2>
<p>If you don't play for a week, you're hidden from the leaderboard and your Elo starts to drop each day until you play again. The first drop comes 8 full days (8 &times; 24 hours) after your last match, then one every 24 hours. It never drops you below the bottom of your rank.</p>
<p>The drop stays on your shown Elo (your place, rank and ladder points) for the rest of the season. Your matches still count you at your Elo before the drop: you're matched with players of that level, and your opponents gain and lose what they would against you at your real level.</p>

<h2 id="season-reset">Seasons and leaderboards</h2>
<p>Each <a href="seasons.html">season</a> ends with a full reset, and everyone plays placement again. The <a href="../leaderboards.html">leaderboards</a> are a 1v1 and a 2v2 Elo ladder.</p>
