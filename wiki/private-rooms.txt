<h2 id="open">Opening and joining</h2>
<ul>
<li>Anyone can open a room, and every room holds up to 64 players.</li>
<li>Players join by invite or with a room code, and anyone in a room can invite.</li>
<li>A room can be played alone, for free.</li>
<li>A party of more than two plays together in a private room.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="formats">What a room can play</h2>
<p>The normal run or a game mode, in one of three formats:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Free-for-all</b> (1v1v1&hellip;),</li>
<li><b>Teams of two</b> (2v2v2&hellip;), racing as in 2v2: the first pair to finish wins,</li>
<li><b>Co-op teams</b>, which all play to the end and are placed by their times.</li>
</ul>
<p>Rooms don't play RSG or All Advancements; Splitstone opens those in your own instance (<a href="categories.html">RSG and AA</a>).</p>
<h2 id="options">Host options</h2>
<ul>
<li>A filtered seed or a set seed. The Seeds row holds the Overworld seed; the button beside it opens the Nether seed and the RNG seed too. The End always uses the Overworld seed.</li>
<li>The Nether seed is filtered when the Overworld seed is filtered. With a set Overworld seed it is that same seed, unless you choose a filtered one. You can also type one.</li>
<li>The RNG seed decides the <a href="seeds-and-luck.html#luck">standardized luck</a>. It is the Overworld seed unless you type one.</li>
<li>Any typed seed stops the room counting. Reroll needs a filtered Overworld seed and keeps a typed Nether or RNG seed.</li>
<li>Which seed types and which bastion types can come up: include or leave out several of either list, or of both. If the pool has no seeds left of a type you chose, another of your types is played and the loading screen says so; if none of them has seeds left, the room says so and won't start, so pick other types. Rooms never play random seeds.</li>
<li>Start at a chosen split, such as Nether entry or the End.</li>
<li>How teams are made: chosen by the host, random, or balanced by Elo, and the relay region for team and co-op rooms.</li>
<li>A time limit that places players by how far they got.</li>
<li>Whether spectators and coaches are allowed.</li>
<li>Team voice and whole-room voice on or off.</li>
<li>A head start, in seconds, for chosen players.</li>
<li>Most of the usual room rules, except any that change luck.</li>
</ul>
<p>Rooms always use Splitstone's <a href="seeds-and-luck.html#luck">standardized luck</a>, so there are no luck switches.</p>
<h2 id="results">Results</h2>
<ul>
<li>Room matches don't change Elo.</li>
<li>They go in a match history of their own, and room times have their own leaderboard, separate from Ranked 1v1.</li>
<li>In a team or co-op room, if the player hosting the shared world leaves or crashes, the room ends; start a new one to keep playing.</li>
<li>When the host of a free-for-all room leaves, the room ends once any running match finishes. A host who wants to leave can hand the host role to another player first, and a host can remove a player from the room.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="supporter">Extras for supporters</h2>
<p>Every host gets the options above. <a href="supporting.html#supporter">Supporter</a> adds a custom room name and a "request to join" mode.</p>
