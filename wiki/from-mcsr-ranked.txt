<p>Splitstone is its own platform, built for players who already know MCSR Ranked: the same 1v1 race on a shared seed, with your own instance, your own Minecraft account and the speedrun.com legal mods you already use. These are the things that work differently. Tap a line for the full article.</p>
<h2 id="setup">Setup</h2>
<ul class="mx">
<li><a href="install.html#own-instance"><b>Its own instance</b><span>Splitstone runs in an instance of its own, beside your Ranked one in the same launcher.</span><small>Ranked won't start next to a mod that isn't on speedrun.com's legal list, and Splitstone's isn't.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="categories.html#switch"><b>Speedrun.com categories</b><span>RSG and the other categories run in your own speedrun.com-legal instance, one click away, so every run there can be submitted.</span><small>Ranked hosts its categories inside private rooms.</small></a></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="rating">Rating and ranks</h2>
<ul class="mx">
<li><a href="elo-and-ranks.html#ranks"><b>Seven ranks and the Top 20</b><span>Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Obsidian and Netherite, and the 20 highest places show Top 20 instead of a rank.</span><small>Ranked has six ranks, from Coal to Netherite.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="elo-and-ranks.html#elo"><b>Faster-settling Elo</b><span>K is 80 for your first 10 matches each season, then 40.</span><small>Ranked uses one fixed K; Splitstone moves to a fixed K of 40 once enough people play.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="elo-and-ranks.html#inactive"><b>Steeper decay, for everyone</b><span>After a week off you're hidden from the leaderboard and lose 12 Elo a day at Netherite, 8 at Obsidian and 5 below, never past the bottom of your rank.</span><small>On Ranked only the top 150 decay, at 5 a day.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="ranked-matches.html#modes"><b>Ranked 2v2</b><span>Two teams of two, with a separate 2v2 Elo and its own ladder. Co-op is a mode too, unrated.</span><small>Ranked is 1v1 only.</small></a></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="season">The season</h2>
<ul class="mx">
<li><a href="seasons.html#shape"><b>16-week seasons with events</b><span>Weekend cups for every rank, three Majors and the playoffs, which alone crown the champion.</span><small>Ranked splits its season into monthly phases.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="seasons.html#active-weeks"><b>Points from weekly places</b><span>Play 10 ranked matches in a week and your place that week earns points; your best 10 of 15 weeks count.</span><small>Ranked awards phase points from a monthly snapshot.</small></a></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="matches">Queues and matches</h2>
<ul class="mx">
<li><a href="matchmaking.html#bots"><b>Bots in the ranked queue</b><span>After a long wait you're offered a bot match, which counts for Elo; at most one in every 5 games. There is no Casual queue.</span><small>Ranked offers bots in Casual only.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="ranked-matches.html#start"><b>A clean start</b><span>Nothing of the world shows or is heard before you spawn.</span><small>Ranked shows the world frozen behind the countdown.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="ranked-matches.html#votes"><b>Reroll and countdowns</b><span>A new seed is a handshake: both players turn on their Reroll light, and the new seed is always a different type. A countdown shows when Reroll, draw and forfeit unlock.</span></a></li>
<li><a href="seeds-and-luck.html#luck"><b>Woodlighting standardized</b><span>Luck follows Ranked's standardization list, plus woodlighting.</span></a></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="around">Around the match</h2>
<ul class="mx">
<li><a href="replays-and-spectating.html#kept"><b>Saved top matches</b><span>Matches where every player is in the top 100 are saved for good, and anyone can download them in the game. Every replay and analysis feature is free.</span><small>Ranked deletes replays after 3 days unless you download them.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="replays-and-spectating.html#coaching"><b>Live watching and coaches</b><span>Anyone can watch ranked matches live with no delay, and a player can bring up to two coaches.</span></a></li>
<li><a href="private-rooms.html#open"><b>Bigger rooms, free solo rooms</b><span>Rooms hold 64 players, can be played alone for free, and offer free-for-all, team and co-op formats, time limits and head starts.</span><small>On Ranked a room holds 32 plus the host, and a solo room is a paid perk.</small></a></li>
<li><a href="social.html#voice"><b>Parties and voice</b><span>Parties of up to 64, and voice for parties, teams, coaches and rooms.</span></a></li>
<li><a href="profiles.html#h2h"><b>Head-to-head and rivals</b><span>Another player's profile opens on your record against them, and yours lists your rivals.</span></a></li>
<li><a href="supporting.html#tiers"><b>Supporter tiers</b><span>$2.49, $4.99 and $7.99 a month. Paying never helps you win.</span></a></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="same">What stays the same</h2>
<ul>
<li>The allowed mods are speedrun.com's legal list, and you can't add Fabric API yourself.</li>
<li>5 placement matches, scored by how far you get, and a full reset each season.</li>
<li>Seeds from a secret filtered pool, with some seed types unlocking by rank.</li>
<li>World resets with no cooldown, draw votes, and live watching with ghosting handled by the rules.</li>
<li>Your Minecraft account is your account, with no separate sign-up.</li>
</ul>
