<div class="wsp" id="wsp" aria-live="polite"><h3>Week 1 &middot; Placements</h3><ul><li>After the reset, everyone plays 5 placement matches first.</li><li>Ranked all week, and 10 ranked matches make the week count for ladder points.</li><li>No cups this weekend: they start in week 2.</li></ul></div>
<ul>
<li><b>All season:</b> ranked play. A week runs from Sunday 9 PM ET to the next Sunday 9 PM ET.</li>
<li><b>Week 1:</b> placements, with no cups.</li>
<li><b>Weeks 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11 and 12:</b> the Netherite cup, from Friday 9 PM to Sunday 9 PM ET. The other ranks' cups run every weekend from week 2.</li>
<li><b>Weeks 4, 8 and 13:</b> Majors.</li>
<li><b>Week 14:</b> a break, and the last week that earns ladder points.</li>
<li><b>Weeks 15 and 16:</b> the playoffs, over two weekends. The season ends after the final.</li>
</ul>
<p>Only Majors, the playoffs and the Netherite cup are events. 2v2 runs the same season system. Co-op has no season, because it is unrated.</p>
<h2 id="active-weeks">Making a week count</h2>
<p>Play <b>10 ranked matches</b> in a week and that week counts: your Elo when the week ends earns ladder points, <b>1 for every 25 Elo above 1800</b> (2100 earns 12, 2600 earns 32), and your cup for that weekend unlocks as soon as your 10th ranked match of the week is played, while the cup is open. Your best 10 of weeks 1 to 14 count. Points from Elo reward how high you climb and how far ahead you are, not just your place. No single snapshot of the ladder decides a playoff place.</p>
<h2 id="top-cup">Weekend cups</h2>
<ul>
<li>Every rank has its own weekend cup. You can enter only the cup for your rank, taken when the cup opens and kept for the weekend.</li>
<li>The top cup is the <b>Netherite cup</b>: every Netherite player and the Top 20. It runs on 9 weekends and is the only cup that gives playoff points; your best 6 of 9 count. The other cups give cosmetic rewards.</li>
<li>A cup is a weekend 1v1 league of <b>7</b> matches at launch, played whenever you're online over the weekend. The beta can change the number.</li>
<li>Every match has its own seed, so no seed can be scouted or shared.</li>
<li>Swiss pairing: each match is against a player on the same cup record, widening by at most one step when nobody is online. Ties go to whoever beat stronger opponents, counted by the cup wins of the players each one beat.</li>
<li>Cup matches don't change Elo and follow the ranked match rules. A draw is half a win, and a player who stops early keeps their record and is placed by it.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="majors">Majors</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>64 players:</b> the top 58 on playoff points, plus 6 standout places for players not already in: 3 for the fastest finishes and 3 for the biggest Elo climbs since the last Major, or since the season's start. There is no open qualifier.</li>
<li><b>Saturday from 12 PM ET:</b> all 64 race the same 5 seeds, with 13 minutes per seed. On each seed every player gets a place, finishers by time and then the rest by how far they got, and scores 65 minus their place.</li>
<li>The top 32 go into a knockout, seeded by race result (1st plays 32nd), and play the rounds of 32 and 16 the same day. A tie at 32nd place is settled by one sudden-death seed.</li>
<li><b>Sunday from 12 PM ET:</b> the last 8 play on to the final.</li>
<li>Knockout matches are best of 3 until the last 8, then best of 5, final included. There are no seed rerolls or forfeits, and a game nobody finishes in 20 minutes goes to the player who got further.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="playoffs">The playoffs</h2>
<p>The top 16 on playoff points when week 14 ends play a single-elimination bracket over two weekends, best of 5 until the best-of-7 final, each day from 12 PM ET: the round of 16 on Saturday of week 15 (all eight series side by side) and the quarterfinals on Sunday, the semifinals on Saturday of week 16 and the final on Sunday. There is no last-chance qualifier, and only the playoffs crown a champion.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Picks:</b> on Monday of week 15 from 2 PM ET, the top 8 on points pick their round-of-16 opponent from the bottom 8, 1st picking first, 2 minutes each, live in the game. A pick made on the show can be entered for a player. A missed pick gets the lowest seed left.</li>
<li>The bracket is fixed: each top-8 seed keeps their place, so 1st and 8th can meet only in the quarterfinals.</li>
<li>The 16 stay on the leaderboard through both weekends, without decay, even if they don't play ranked.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="prizes">Prizes</h2>
<p>The playoffs pay a cash prize; Majors pay none. The amount is announced when it is set.</p>
<h2 id="season-end">When a season ends</h2>
<ul>
<li>Ratings fully reset, and everyone plays <a href="elo-and-ranks.html#placement">placement</a> again.</li>
<li>Season-end titles: special titles for 1st, 2nd and 3rd, then top 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100.</li>
<li>Every player gets a recap card to share.</li>
<li>Each battle pass follows a season, and rewards not reached by its end are lost.</li>
</ul>
