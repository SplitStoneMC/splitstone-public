<h2 id="queues">Queues</h2>
<ul>
<li>Every mode has its own queue: Ranked 1v1, Ranked 2v2, and each game mode while it is on the dashboard.</li>
<li>There is no 1v1 Casual queue. To play casually, join a <a href="public-rooms.html">public room</a> from Extra Modes: no Elo moves there.</li>
<li>Queueing runs in the background. While you wait you can sit in a private room, play your own practice world, or do anything else that doesn't put you against another person.</li>
<li>You can leave the queue at any time before a match is found, from the queue screen or with <b>Ctrl+Z</b> from anywhere (change the key in Controls). After that it can't be cancelled or declined.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="pairing">Who you play</h2>
<p>You're paired by Elo and by how often you forfeit. Close Elo comes first, and the allowed gap widens the longer you wait.</p>
<p>The starting numbers, which will be tuned with real players: the search starts at &plusmn;50 Elo and widens steadily to &plusmn;250 by 2:30, then to &plusmn;450 by 5:30, and keeps widening at that pace until you find a match. You can also set your matchmaking speed to fast, balanced or accurate; it starts on balanced.</p>
<h2 id="teams">2v2</h2>
<ul>
<li>A 2v2 team's strength is the plain average of its two players' 2v2 Elo. A premade duo can be matched against two random players.</li>
<li>1v1 is one global pool, because each player plays their own world. In 2v2 and Swap only teammates are matched by region, because they share a world through a relay there; a 2v2 team can meet a team from any region.</li>
<li>Your region is the nearest of four (North America, Europe, Asia and Oceania), found automatically, and you can pick any of them in settings. The Middle East has no server yet, so players there play on Europe. Queueing alone, you can turn on "join the global pool" to find a teammate anywhere. In a party, the leader picks the region.</li>
<li>Players you've blocked are never given to you as a random teammate.</li>
</ul>
<p>Swap can also be played by one duo alone, against the clock and its best times, with no opposing team.</p>
<h2 id="forfeits">Forfeits and queue bans</h2>
<p>Three forfeits in a row give a 3-minute queue ban, and it grows with each further forfeit in a row. Your forfeit rate also counts in pairing. Each queue keeps its own forfeit streak, queue ban and forfeit rate, so a ban in one queue doesn't keep you out of another.</p>
<h2 id="busy">Busy times</h2>
<p>If players arrive faster than the service can take them, you see a "you're in line" screen until there's room.</p>
