<h2 id="what-you-need">What you need</h2>
<ul>
<li>A Minecraft Java Edition account.</li>
<li>One of the supported launchers below, on Windows, macOS or Linux.</li>
</ul>
<p>There is no Splitstone launcher. Your launcher downloads the game from Mojang and signs you in with Microsoft as it always does, so Splitstone never handles your password or copies game files.</p>

<h2 id="packs">Choose a pack</h2>
<p>Splitstone comes as one ready-made pack or as the mod on its own, for Windows, macOS and Linux. There is no practice pack with reset mods: <a href="practice.html">practice</a> has its own button for a new world.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Splitstone Pack:</b> Splitstone plus legal mods: the performance mods Sodium (its Mac build on macOS), Lithium, Starlight, LazyDFU and Krypton; SpeedrunAPI, which that Sodium build needs to start; Voyager and Antigone, which fix rare crashes and freezes; StandardSettings, which resets your options at the start of each match; LazyStronghold, which builds strongholds faster; and AntiResourceReload, which makes new worlds open faster.</li>
<li><b>The mod alone:</b> for players who already have their own setup. Only mods on the legal mods list are allowed (<a href="faq.html#own-mods">details</a>).</li>
</ul>
<p>Download them from the <a href="../download.html">download page</a>: one pack file for each of Windows, macOS and Linux, which Prism, MultiMC and the Modrinth App all import. The pack fetches each mod from the legal mods list on GitHub rather than copying it.</p>
<h2 id="own-instance">Give it its own instance</h2>
<p>Splitstone runs in its own instance. Other speedrun setups often refuse to start next to mods they don't expect, and some bundle their own copies of mods that Splitstone's packs include. Make a new instance for Splitstone and leave your other instances as they are; they can all sit side by side in the same launcher.</p>
<h2 id="steps">Install in four steps</h2>
<ol class="steps">
<li><span><b>Open a supported launcher.</b>Sign in with your Microsoft account as usual.</span></li>
<li><span><b>Add Splitstone Pack as a new instance.</b>Download the pack file for your system and import it.</span></li>
<li><span><b>Launch the instance.</b>Splitstone starts inside Minecraft.</span></li>
<li><span><b>Finish the first-launch screen.</b>See below.</span></li>
</ol>
<p>The <a href="../download.html">download page</a> has the steps for each launcher.</p>
<h2 id="first-launch">Your first launch</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Agree to the <a href="../rules.html">rules</a>, the <a href="../terms.html">terms</a> and the <a href="../privacy.html">privacy policy</a>.</b> You can't play without agreeing.</li>
<li><b>Say how old you are: under 13, 13–17, or 18 or older.</b> Players must be 13 or older; 13–17 year olds keep the chat filter on. Splitstone never asks for or keeps your birth date, and you can change your answer in Settings.</li>
<li><b>Choose who can send you friend requests:</b> everyone, friends of friends and people you've played with, or nobody. You can change it later.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="sign-in">Signing in</h2>
<p>You don't make a Splitstone account or password. Your Minecraft account is your Splitstone account. When the game starts, it asks Microsoft for a signed player certificate, and Splitstone's service checks that certificate. Your login token never leaves your computer.</p>
<p>Because the certificate comes from Microsoft, Splitstone can't sign you in while Microsoft's service is down.</p>
<h2 id="updates">Updates</h2>
<p>Only the latest version of Splitstone can connect, so everyone plays the same rules. When a new version comes out, the game's built-in updater installs it.</p>
<h2 id="problems">If something goes wrong</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>"Splitstone: can't reach the service."</b> Check your internet connection, then the <a href="../status.html">status page</a>. The game keeps trying on its own and connects once the service answers.</li>
<li><b>"Your Minecraft account could not be confirmed."</b> Sign out of your Microsoft account in your launcher, sign in again and restart the game. If it keeps happening, Microsoft's service may be down: check <a href="https://support.xbox.com/xbox-live-status">Xbox status</a>.</li>
<li><b>"Could not set up a secure connection."</b> Check that your computer's date and time are right. Antivirus or firewall programs that inspect web traffic can also cause it.</li>
<li><b>An update could not be downloaded or installed.</b> Restart the game to try again. If it still fails, download the latest pack from the <a href="../download.html">download page</a>.</li>
<li><b>The game closes or crashes on start.</b> Make sure Splitstone has its own instance made from Splitstone Pack, with no mods added to it. Don't add Fabric API. After a crash, the next launch asks whether to send the crash report; saying yes helps us fix it.</li>
<li><b>The launcher says Java is missing.</b> Let your launcher download it: Prism and the Modrinth App can download the right Java for 1.16.1 from their settings.</li>
</ul>
<p>Still stuck? Ask in #install-help on our <a href="https://discord.gg/wxDcZ8NGWt">Discord</a> with your launcher, your operating system and a screenshot of the error, or email contact@splitstonemc.com. For a bug in the game, use "Report a bug" in game; it sends your logs with the report.</p>
