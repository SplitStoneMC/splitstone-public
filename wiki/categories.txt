<h2 id="why">A shortcut, not a Splitstone mode</h2>
<p>RSG and All Advancements aren't played in Splitstone. Splitstone's mod changes luck for its own matches, so it isn't allowed in runs you submit to speedrun.com. Instead, Splitstone gives you a shortcut: it opens your own legal instance for you, untouched, and every run there can be submitted as usual.</p>
<h2 id="switch">How the switch works</h2>
<ol class="steps">
<li><span><b>Pick "Play RSG" or "Play AA".</b>It's in Splitstone's menu.</span></li>
<li><span><b>Splitstone closes and your legal instance starts.</b>Your launcher opens it as it is: no Splitstone mod, no cosmetics, vanilla luck.</span></li>
<li><span><b>Quit that game and Splitstone starts again.</b>This happens only when you came from Splitstone. It can also bring back the run's SpeedRunIGT record after the run.</span></li>
</ol>
<p>A small helper waits for your legal game to close, whether you quit or it crashed, and starts Splitstone again. Splitstone never edits your launcher's files. Each switch restarts the game.</p>
<div class="co"><span><b>Still being tested.</b> The one-click switch has to pass hand tests on each supported launcher before it is finished, so details may change.</span></div>
<h2 id="launchers">Launchers</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Prism, MultiMC, the Modrinth App and CurseForge:</b> covered if their tests pass, as is any other launcher that can start an instance by command.</li>
<li><b>Official Minecraft Launcher:</b> it can't start a chosen installation for Splitstone, so you switch by hand.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="records">Records</h2>
<p>Splitstone keeps your RSG and AA times for your stats. Runs you submit are judged where you submit them, under those rules.</p>
