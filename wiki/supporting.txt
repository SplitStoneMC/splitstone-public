<h2 id="rule">What stays free or earned only</h2>
<ul>
<li>Ranked, the practice tools and replay analysis are free for everyone.</li>
<li>Rank marks, such as gold, the crown, medals and the lava glow, can only be earned. A shop item never looks like competitive status.</li>
<li>Bought cosmetics never appear on leaderboard rows, only on profiles and hover cards.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="supporter">Supporter</h2>
<p>Supporter is <b>$3.99</b> a month. It adds:</p>
<ul>
<li>A supporter badge on your name and an animated name colour.</li>
<li>Up to 8 highlighted achievements on your profile, instead of 5.</li>
<li>Supporter profile banners and profile themes.</li>
<li>A custom name and a "request to join" mode for your private rooms.</li>
<li>Supporter chat on our Discord.</li>
<li>Your help requests, reports and appeals are looked at first. Rules and outcomes are the same for everyone.</li>
<li>The battle pass's paid track. Its items stay yours only if you're still a Supporter when the season ends.</li>
</ul>
<p>Supporter is sold on the <a href="../shop.html">Shop page</a>.</p>
<h2 id="buying">Buying on the website</h2>
<ul>
<li>No sign-in: type the Minecraft username of the player it is for, and choose whether it's a gift.</li>
<li>Before you pay, the website shows that player's head, name and profile so you can check it's the right person.</li>
<li>Supporter can go to any Minecraft account, even one that has never signed in to Splitstone. The website then shows "not on Splitstone yet", and Supporter waits for that player's first sign-in.</li>
<li>Payment goes through Tebex, which tells Splitstone's service to add Supporter to that Minecraft account.</li>
<li>A gift is paid once and lasts a month; it never renews. When it ends, the player receiving it can subscribe themselves.</li>
<li>From 16 you can buy for yourself; younger players get Supporter when a parent buys it.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="cosmetics">Cosmetics</h2>
<ul>
<li>Cosmetics and coins are sold in the game's own shop, where you can try items on your 3D model first. The website sells only Supporter.</li>
<li>Kinds of cosmetic at launch: hats (halos and horns included), masks and glasses, wings, back items, emotes, win effects, titles, banners and name colours. Shoulder pets, outfits, tails and moving wings come later. Never capes, or anything shaped like a cape or an elytra.</li>
<li>Outfits are drawn over your own skin and never change your Minecraft skin.</li>
<li>Your equipped set shows everywhere: matches, ranked included, replays and profiles. Other players' cosmetics can be hidden only in replays, with the Show Cosmetics switch. Your own RSG and AA instance shows none.</li>
<li>The shop rotates: items are on sale in limited windows. Some return, some never do, and bundles cost fewer coins.</li>
<li>Coins have no real-world value and can't be cashed out or transferred.</li>
<li>Join the creators' channel on our <a href="https://discord.gg/wxDcZ8NGWt" rel="noopener">Discord</a> to learn how listing your cosmetics on Splitstone works.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="earned">Earned items</h2>
<ul>
<li>Some cosmetics can only be earned: through the battle pass, playtime and levels, achievements and wins. Earned items are yours for good.</li>
<li>Being anonymous to opponents during matches is a rare battle pass reward, earned by playing.</li>
<li>The battle pass is free for everyone. Its paid track comes with Supporter. Each pass follows a <a href="seasons.html">season</a>. Free rewards not reached by its end can come back only in a later pass, never in the shop.</li>
<li>You can gift many bought items, and the person receiving one can accept or decline it. Earned items can't be gifted.</li>
</ul>
