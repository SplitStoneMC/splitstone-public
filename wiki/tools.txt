<h2 id="allowed">What is allowed</h2>
<p>Programs that run beside the game follow speedrun.com's rules: its approved calculators, such as Ninjabrain Bot, and its approved Toolscreen builds. Mods inside the game have their own rule (<a href="faq.html#own-mods">mods and tools</a>).</p>
<p>The two tools below are the ones most runners use. Neither is part of Splitstone Pack: you install them yourself, and their authors, not Splitstone, look after them.</p>
<h2 id="ninjabrain-bot">Ninjabrain Bot</h2>
<p>A stronghold calculator. It works out where the stronghold is from your eye throws.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>How you use it:</b> throw an eye of ender, look straight at it and press F3+C. Move somewhere else and do it again. The calculator lists the likely stronghold locations and how sure it is of each.</li>
<li><b>What it runs on:</b> it is a Java program, a single .jar file that you open.</li>
<li><b>Cost:</b> free and open source.</li>
<li><b>Get it:</b> <a href="https://github.com/Ninjabrain1/Ninjabrain-Bot/releases/latest">Ninjabrain Bot's releases on GitHub</a>. Its <a href="https://github.com/Ninjabrain1/Ninjabrain-Bot">page</a> links video guides.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="toolscreen">Toolscreen</h2>
<p>A program for playing in fullscreen: it resizes the game with a hotkey (thin, wide), magnifies the area around your crosshair for reading eye throws (EyeZoom), and shows other windows, such as Ninjabrain Bot, on top of the game.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>What it runs on:</b> Windows 10 and 11 only. Of the launchers Splitstone supports, it works with Prism Launcher and MultiMC.</li>
<li><b>How you install it:</b> download its installer, copy it into your Splitstone instance's folder and open it there. Its own page has the steps.</li>
<li><b>Antivirus:</b> Toolscreen works by loading itself into the game, so Windows Defender or another antivirus may warn about it. Its installer asks you to allow it.</li>
<li><b>Cost:</b> free and open source.</li>
<li><b>Get it:</b> <a href="https://github.com/jojoe77777/Toolscreen/releases/latest">Toolscreen's releases on GitHub</a>. Its <a href="https://github.com/jojoe77777/Toolscreen">page</a> has the install steps and a video guide.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="help">Help with a tool</h2>
<p>For a problem with one of these tools, ask its author's community, linked from the tool's page. For a problem with Splitstone itself, see <a href="install.html#problems">if something goes wrong</a>.</p>
