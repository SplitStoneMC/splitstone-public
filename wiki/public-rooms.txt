<h2 id="joining">Joining</h2>
<ul>
<li>Public rooms are in Extra Modes. Anyone can join, and a room holds up to 24 players; when every room is full, joining opens another.</li>
<li>No Elo moves and nothing is at stake: leaving or giving up a seed costs nothing.</li>
<li>You can queue Ranked 1v1, Ranked 2v2 or a game mode while you're in a public room. A match found comes first: you leave the current seed and are back in the room afterwards.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="seeds">Seeds</h2>
<ul>
<li>Once two players are in, a seed starts a minute after the last one ended. Seeds are ranked seeds from the pool.</li>
<li>Everyone plays until the seed ends: after its first finish, the others get extra time (12 minutes below Gold, 10 from Gold to Diamond, 8 from Diamond up), or the seed ends when half its players have finished.</li>
<li>If nobody finishes, the seed ends after 40 minutes below Gold, 30 from Gold to Diamond, or 25 from Diamond up, and places go by how far players got.</li>
<li>Between seeds, and while you wait after joining mid-seed, you practise in your own world. The next seed pulls you in.</li>
<li>If your game drops, you're out of that seed and keep your place in the room for 2 minutes.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="points">Places and points</h2>
<ul>
<li>Places follow finishing order. A seed's win is 1 point, and seven seeds make a round; the round's winner is shown and its points start again, with the day's points kept beside them. Days are counted in UTC.</li>
<li>Each seed shows its firsts: the first player into the Nether, a bastion, a fortress, the stronghold and the End, and the first to finish. A switch shows who was first most often over the day.</li>
<li>Public rooms keep no standings beyond the day.</li>
</ul>
